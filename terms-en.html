Terms of use

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · English translation added 11 October 2026

These are the rules for using Vinventory. In short: it is a free hobby project, it can break, and your data stays yours.

This is a translation of the Dutch version. If the two differ, the Dutch text applies.

1. What is Vinventory?

A free app for keeping track of your wine cellar, offered by a private individual as a hobby project. It is not a web shop and nothing is sold.

2. Who is it for?

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over and for personal use.

3. Your account

  • Choose a password that you don't use anywhere else, and don't share it.
  • An account is for one person. You are responsible for what happens with your account.
  • You can always delete your account yourself (Settings, Account, Delete account).

4. Your data

Your wines, photos and notes stay yours. You only give the app permission to store and show them for you. Don't put unlawful content in it, and take care with data of other people (such as names in your logbook). How data is handled is described in the privacy statement.

5. Scanning with Claude

This is an extra feature that only works with your own Anthropic key. The costs are charged to your Anthropic account. Set a limit there yourself. Share your key with no one. The administrator of Vinventory is not responsible for costs on your account.

6. Fair use

Don't use the app to bother others, to get at other people's data, or to overload the service, for example with automated bulk use.

Friends and tips. Only invite people you know. Don't send hurtful, unlawful or misleading content, including in the message with a tip. You can decline a tip you receive; someone who bothers you can be blocked and reported. In case of misuse an account can be removed.

7. No warranty

  • The app is provided as it is. It may have faults, be temporarily unavailable or contain errors. The free database may, for example, pause.
  • Data can be lost. So make a backup yourself now and then (Settings, then Backup).
  • Descriptions, drinking windows and dishes that Claude suggests are guidelines and may be wrong.

8. Liability

To the extent the law allows, the administrator is not liable for damage resulting from the use of, or the inability to use, the app, except in case of intent or gross negligence. Rights that cannot be excluded by law remain.

9. Changes and stopping

The administrator can change the app or stop it. If that happens, this will be announced on the website beforehand where possible, so that you can make a backup. In case of misuse an account can be removed.

10. Applicable law

Dutch law applies to these terms.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Vinventory/vinventory.github.io/issues. Issues on GitHub are public, so don't put personal data in them.
